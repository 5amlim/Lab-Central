# Clinical content review

Lab Central is a visual reference and training aid. It does not validate live instrument status, issue clinical decisions, or replace the applicable manufacturer IFU, laboratory SOP, or medical director-approved protocol.

Review points carried forward from earlier project documentation:

- **PATHFAST chest-pain pathways:** Earlier reference materials differed in their delta-threshold notation and initial rule-out criteria. Confirm the current approved assay-specific algorithm before using the site for patient-care guidance.
- **Timed troponin draws:** Collection windows and missed-window handling must reflect the approved local protocol. Record the actual collection time rather than inferring it from the scheduled time.
- **Specimen interference:** Any same-tube plasma retest for suspected fibrin or microclot interference is an investigation under the applicable SOP, not a replacement for an ordered serial specimen or clinical escalation.
- **Controls and calibration:** Review assay-specific control materials, storage, frequency, and analyzer lane assignments against current lot documents and procedures.
- **Sendout tests:** Verify test codes, specimen requirements, transport stability, pickup times, and payer restrictions with current receiving-laboratory instructions.

The site includes simplified equipment and analyzer-screen illustrations. They are visual references, not exact firmware screenshots or automated readings from instruments.

Protocol review should be documented by the appropriate clinical and laboratory leadership before a new or substantially changed workflow is released for staff use.
